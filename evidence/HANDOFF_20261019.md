## Delivered contracts

The [current inventory](GOOGLE_COMPLETE_INVENTORY.md) and
[pinned source/API/test map](SOURCE_TEST_MAP.md) are the review entry points.
They distinguish each operation's path/memory/fd-range inputs and actual outputs,
public facade versus internal MP4 helper use, malformed versus unsupported
layouts, and metadata declarations versus verified media. The initial core
matrix is retained as a historical receipt.

* Core keeps the existing resource/pairing/provenance/plan-execute model, exact
  integer time and per-resource disposition. The default rejects unmapped
  resources; sidecar preservation and explicit dropping remain caller choices.
* JPEG/HEIC/AVIF directory/mpvd/XMP/resource extraction and building retain
  encoded images, movies and supported auxiliary bytes. Google HEIF pixel
  re-encoding is intentionally not copied. Existing Apple support remains;
  optional color/video checks do not modify preserved inputs.
* Raw XMP, complete supported AGTM-v0 fields and existing score/flag descriptors
  are exposed separately from rendering. Unit-rate empty/trim/repeat timing is
  exact. Explicit scoring-media-CTS projection requires caller-selected video
  tracks and never guesses the payload's time domain or a nearest frame.
* Optional H05 and G08/G09 reports separate violations, unsupported facts and
  recommendations. Orientation, coded dimensions, color/HDR signaling and
  sample-entry/AAC declarations are facts with documented interpretation limits.
* Supported MicroVideo, Samsung, Oplus and Honor provider layouts have concrete
  tests and boundaries. The [five-vendor research ledger](VENDOR_RESEARCH_20261019.md)
  adds CameraAlbum raw inspection verified on both existing vivo originals;
  matching IDs do not prove pairing and imageTime units remain unverified.
* Explicit bounded track import/deletion, metadata-track inspection, fast-start
  relocation and compaction retain packet/timing checks and reports. Google's
  hvc1 optimization guard and copied-track timing difference are documented;
  Google's output is not an unconditional correctness oracle.

Maintained CLI/C/JNI/Android and existing Apple/browser adapters have separate
integration gates. Their presence does not claim C++/Google-Java ABI equivalence
or complete ecosystem/device compatibility. Moments construction is excluded.
